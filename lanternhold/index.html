Formation tips

    Campfire

    Companions resting by the fire work camp jobs that help the whole party, even while you are deep below. Train them here to make their jobs count for more.

    Trading PostBuy and sell gear with other players, and send items by mail.

    Rekindle the Lantern

    Return to the surface and pour what you learned into the lantern. Gold, floors and training reset. You gain Embers, each adding +10% damage and gold forever, and Kindling to spend on your skill tree, bag space and extra gear slots. Your companions, gear, relics, story and feats are kept.

    You would gain 0 Embers and 0 Kindling

    Reach floor 50 to rekindle.

    Guardians on floor 10 and below sometimes drop a relic. Relics stay with you forever, and a duplicate raises its level.

    Feats each adds +2% party damage
