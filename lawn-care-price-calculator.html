Questions

How do I find my mowing speed?

Measure a few lawns with a free online map tool, time how long the mowing takes (not the trimming), and divide the area by the hours.

Why include drive time?

An hour on the road is an hour you can't bill. Building it into each visit keeps your price honest.

What about leaf cleanup or fertilizing?

Price those as separate add-ons. Use the hourly rate calculator to set a fair hourly price for them.
