Jobber pricing explained

What each Jobber plan costs, what you really get, and the extra costs people miss, written for one- to five-person service businesses.

How we make money: some links to software on this page may be referral links. If you sign up through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend, and we tell you when a spreadsheet is the better choice. Prices checked November 2026 from each company's pricing page.

The plans at a glance

PlanBilled yearlyMonth to monthWhat it adds
Core$29/mo$49/mo1 user. Scheduling, quotes, invoices, card payments, online booking.
Connect$99/mo$139/moAdds QuickBooks Online sync and more automation. A common pick for small crews.
Grow$149/mo$199/moAdds review requests and more marketing tools.

Prices are per month in US dollars, from Jobber's pricing page in November 2026. Jobber also offers a larger plan for bigger teams. Check Jobber's pricing page for current prices before you buy.

The costs people miss

  • Extra users. Core includes one user. Each extra person is about $29 a month, so a crew of three adds roughly $58.
  • Card processing. Card payments cost 2.9% + 30¢ each, and bank (ACH) payments 1%. On $8,000 a month of card payments that's about $230, often more than the plan itself.
  • Monthly vs yearly. Paying yearly saves $20 to $50 a month, but you pay up front. Use the 14-day trial and a few monthly payments before you commit.

Which plan should you pick?

  • Solo, under about 15 jobs a week: Core is enough. If admin takes less than three hours a week, a spreadsheet may still be the cheaper choice.
  • You use QuickBooks, or quotes slip through the cracks: Connect. Compare its automation features on the trial, since that's where most time savings come from.
  • You want more Google reviews and upsells: Grow, but only once your schedule is steady.

Questions

Does Jobber have a free plan?

No, but it has a 14-day free trial with no card needed.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can change plans as your business grows. If you paid yearly, check the terms before you switch.
