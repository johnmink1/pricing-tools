Lanternhold privacy

Last updated November 23, 2026.

Playing as a guest

If you don't sign in, your progress is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us. There are no ads, analytics or tracking scripts in the game.

Creating an account

An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email address and your game save so you can load your progress on any device. Your password is handled by Amazon Cognito and is never seen or stored by us. Accounts and saves are kept on Amazon Web Services in the United States. We never sell or share this information, and we only email you the codes you ask for, such as to confirm your email or reset your password.

Signing in with Born 2 Game

If you use Continue with Born 2 Game or Get Sigils, you sign in on the Born 2 Game store, which is run on Wix. The game receives a sign-in token for your store account and keeps it in your browser. We use it to ask the store who you are and which Sigil packs you have bought. We store your store member ID, your store nickname and the order IDs of your packs, so each pack is credited once and to one game account. If your store account has no game account yet, we create one with a placeholder address that is never emailed. Payments are handled entirely by the store; we never see your card or payment details.

Sigils, the Lantern Pass and quests

We keep your Sigil balance, the looks you own, your Lantern Pass end date, and which daily quests you have claimed today, so they stay with your account.

Mercy's Blessing

The game reads only the public donation totals from the Grand War website. No information about who donated is sent to or kept by Lanternhold.

Multiplayer and chat

In Multiplayer, your hero's name, class, banner, level and deepest floor are shown on the hero board so other players can recruit it. Chat messages are shown to other signed-in players with your hero's name, kept for 7 days, and then deleted automatically. Banner chat is shown only to players who fly the same banner. A player is identified in chat only by an anonymous code, never by email. Messages that several players report are hidden.

Deleting your data

While signed in, open Settings and choose Erase progress. That deletes the save stored with your account. To have your account, store link and purchase records removed as well, contact us through the support link in the game.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when the fonts load.
