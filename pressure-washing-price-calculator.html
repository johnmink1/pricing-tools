How to set your per-square-foot rate

Rates vary a lot by region and surface, so don't copy a number off the internet. Call or check three local competitors, then use this calculator to make sure that price still leaves you a good hourly rate after costs. The surface multiplier above is a starting point for harder, slower surfaces; change your base rate to match your market.

Always use a minimum charge

Small jobs still cost you setup, travel and cleanup. A minimum charge stops a 200 sq ft patio from losing money.

Track every job, not just this one

Quoting is step one. The tracker logs every job, flags the ones that paid under your target hourly rate, and totals each month.
