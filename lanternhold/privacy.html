Lanternhold privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Playing as a guest

If you don't sign in, your progress is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us. There are no ads, analytics or tracking scripts in the game.

Signing in

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, we store your name, email address, a Google account ID and your game save, so you can load your progress on any device. We use Google Firebase to sign you in and store your save. We never sell or share this information, and we don't email you.

Deleting your data

While signed in, open Settings and choose Erase progress. That deletes the save stored with your account. To have your sign-in record removed too, contact us through the support link in the game.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when the fonts load.
