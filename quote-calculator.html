Why ask for a deposit?

A deposit of 20% to 50% cuts last-minute cancellations and covers materials you buy ahead of the job. Say on the quote when the balance is due, for example on the day the work is finished.

Quotes that win more jobs

  • Send it the same day. The first clear quote a customer gets often wins.
  • List each service on its own line so the customer can see what they're paying for.
  • Say how long the price is valid, such as 30 days, so old quotes don't come back at old prices.
  • Follow up after three days. Many lost quotes were simply forgotten.

Questions

Do I have to charge sales tax on services?

It depends on your state and the service. Some states tax cleaning or lawn care and others don't. Check your state's revenue department or ask a tax professional.

Why is there a taxable share?

Some jobs mix taxed and untaxed work, such as taxable materials with untaxed labor. Enter the share of the quote that's taxable and the tax is worked out on that part only.
