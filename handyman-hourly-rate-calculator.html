What hourly rate should I charge?

Start from the yearly take-home you want and work backwards. Most owners forget unbilled time (driving, quoting, admin) and end up underpriced.

How this works

Revenue needed = (take-home ÷ (1 − tax %)) + business costs. Divide that by the hours you can actually bill. If you work 45 hours but only 65% are on the job, you bill about 29 hours a week. Your rate has to cover all 45.

Common mistakes

  • Pricing off an employee wage. You also pay for the truck, the insurance and the hours spent quoting.
  • Ignoring slow seasons. Use the weeks you really work.
  • Never raising prices. Rerun this every year as costs rise.
