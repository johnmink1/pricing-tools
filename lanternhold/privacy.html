Lanternhold privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Playing as a guest

If you don't sign in, your progress is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us. There are no ads, analytics or tracking scripts in the game.

Creating an account

An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email address and your game save so you can load your progress on any device. Your password is handled by Amazon Cognito and is never seen or stored by us. Accounts and saves are kept on Amazon Web Services in the United States. We never sell or share this information, and we only email you the codes you ask for, such as to confirm your email or reset your password.

Deleting your data

While signed in, open Settings and choose Erase progress. That deletes the save stored with your account. To have your account removed as well, contact us through the support link in the game.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when the fonts load.
