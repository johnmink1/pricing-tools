Choosing field service software

Scheduling, quotes, invoices and payments in one app can save hours a week. Here's how to tell whether you need it yet, and how to pick one.

How we make money: some links to software on this page may be referral links. If you sign up through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend, and we tell you when a spreadsheet is the better choice. Prices checked November 2026 from each company's pricing page.

Do you need software yet?

A simple rule: if admin work (quoting, scheduling, invoicing and chasing payments) takes you more than three or four hours a week, software will probably pay for itself. If it's less, a spreadsheet and a payment app are fine for now. Our cost calculator works it out from your own numbers.

What to look for

  • Online booking and quotes customers can approve from their phone.
  • Automatic reminders and follow-ups, which is where most time savings come from.
  • Card and bank payments on the invoice. Compare processing rates, not just the monthly fee.
  • QuickBooks sync if your bookkeeper uses it. It's usually on the mid-tier plan.
  • Fair per-user pricing if you have, or plan to hire, a crew.
