Setting your per-square-foot rate

Rates vary a lot by area and surface, so don't copy a number from the internet. Look up what three local competitors charge, then use this calculator to check that the price still leaves you a good hourly rate after costs. The surface options raise the price for slower jobs like siding and decks. Adjust your base rate to fit your market.

Always set a minimum charge

Questions

What does the surface option do?

It multiplies your base rate for surfaces that take longer or need soft washing. The values are starting points, so change your base rate to match local prices.

Should I include travel time?

Yes. Put drive time into your hours on site so your earnings per hour stay honest.

Can I use this for commercial jobs?

Yes, but larger commercial jobs usually get a lower rate per square foot. Lower the base rate and check the earnings per hour.
