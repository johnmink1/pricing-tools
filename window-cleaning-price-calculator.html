Why price by the pane?

Counting panes is quick, easy for customers to understand, and scales fairly from a small ranch house to a big two-story. A French window with many small panes takes longer, so count those panes separately or charge more for them.

Charge for what slows you down

  • Inside cleaning means moving furniture, laying drop cloths and working around people. Many cleaners charge nearly double.
  • Upper floors need ladders or a water-fed pole, so add a fixed amount per pane.
  • Screens and tracks are quick add-ons customers often say yes to.

Questions

What counts as a pane?

One separate piece of glass. A double-hung window usually has two panes, top and bottom. Count each side once and let the inside option handle the second side.

How much should I charge per pane?

It varies a lot by area. Check what local window cleaners charge, then make sure your price per hour on this page meets your target.

Should I offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. Quarterly or twice-a-year plans give you steady work. A small discount is fine if your price per hour still holds up.
