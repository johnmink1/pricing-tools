How to read the result

We take your weekly admin hours, apply the share software typically saves, and value that time at your hourly rate. Then we add profit from any extra jobs that online booking and faster quotes win you, and subtract the monthly fee. Be honest about the extra jobs: if you're already fully booked, enter zero.
