Questions

How fast should I be cleaning?

It depends on the home and the checklist. Time your next three standard cleans and divide square feet by hours.

Should two cleaners cost more?

Usually not. Two cleaners finish faster, but the total labor hours, and so the price, stay about the same.

What's a fair recurring discount?

Many cleaners offer 5% to 15% off. Use the calculator to make sure your discounted price still meets your target pay.
