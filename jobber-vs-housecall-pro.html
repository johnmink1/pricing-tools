Jobber vs Housecall Pro

A plain comparison for one- to five-person service businesses: what each costs, what you get on the cheaper plans, and when neither is worth it yet.

The short answer

  • On a tight budget and working alone: Jobber's Core plan is the cheapest way in, at $29 a month billed yearly or $49 month to month.
  • Want reviews and customer financing from day one: Housecall Pro's Basic plan includes both, at $59 a month billed yearly or $79 month to month.
  • Need QuickBooks sync: Jobber gets you there for less (Connect, $99 a month yearly) than Housecall Pro (Essentials, $149 a month yearly).
  • Doing fewer than about ten jobs a week: a good spreadsheet and a payment app may be all you need for now.

Where Jobber is stronger

Jobber's entry price is lower, and its plans step up in smaller jumps, so you pay for features as you need them. Quote and invoice follow-ups, automatic payment collection and QuickBooks sync arrive on the Connect plan, which is cheaper than Housecall Pro's equivalent.

Where Housecall Pro is stronger

Housecall Pro puts review management and consumer financing on its cheapest plan, which helps if your jobs are larger tickets or you rely on online reviews to win work. Its card rate starts a little lower. Routes and QuickBooks sync come on Essentials.

When to skip both for now

Software earns its keep by saving admin time and getting you paid faster. If you're doing a handful of jobs a week, the monthly fee may cost more than the time it saves. Run your numbers through the software cost calculator before you commit, and use the free trials, which need no card.

Questions

Is this comparison sponsored?

No company paid for this page. Some links may be referral links, which means we may earn a commission if you sign up, at no cost to you. We only use numbers from each company's own pricing page.

Which is better for lawn care?

Both handle recurring visits and routes. Solo mowers on a budget usually start cheaper on Jobber. Crews that want built-in reviews and financing often prefer Housecall Pro. Try both free trials with a real week of jobs.

Which is better for house cleaning?

For a solo cleaner, the cheaper plan of either works. Pick the one whose booking page and reminders your clients find easiest, and check the cost against the time it saves you.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Both let you export customers and jobs, and both offer onboarding help on their larger plans.
